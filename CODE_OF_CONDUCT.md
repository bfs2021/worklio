# Code of Conduct

Worklio adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part in the
project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, chat and any other project space. This page is a
short summary in our own words. If the two differ, the Contributor Covenant wins.

## Our pledge

We want Worklio to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different views and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information (such as an address or email) without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public (for
example from an official account or at an event).

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers using the
contact listed on the GitHub repository page (or the maintainers' GitHub profiles). A dedicated
conduct email address will be added here. Please do not report conduct issues in public issues.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will keep the reporter's identity
private. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who
does, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
